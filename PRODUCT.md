# Half an Onion

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Household members plan what to eat together and take a shared shopping list to individual store branches. Each person has a dietary profile and can participate in different meals.

## Product purpose

The week is the starting point. Members can plan a meal, put a recipe on the calendar, check pantry stock, or record a grocery purchase. Suggested groceries and meals need a household member’s approval.

## Operating context

The kitchen is available on desktop and as an installable phone app. Signed-in members can shop and plan offline after their first online visit. Queued changes, conflicts, and the action to recover them must remain visible. A household can connect ChatGPT for suggestions, text or voice instructions, and recipe capture.

## Product principles

Use the household’s names for people, recipes and stores. Give frequent actions short, visible labels. Reserve explanatory text for decisions, constraints, and changes in application state.
